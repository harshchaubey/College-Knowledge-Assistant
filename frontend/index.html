How it works

Retrieval-Augmented Generation, in four steps

Uploading builds a searchable index once. Every question then searches that index before the AI writes an answer.

  1. 1

    Upload

    The PDF is saved and its text is extracted one page at a time.

    On upload
  2. 2

    Chunk & embed

    Text is split into overlapping chunks, turned into vectors with Sentence Transformers, and stored in ChromaDB.

    On upload
  3. 3

    Retrieve

    Your question becomes a vector too, and the most similar chunks are pulled from ChromaDB.

    On every question
  4. 4

    Answer

    Gemini writes an answer from those chunks and returns it with document and page citations.

    On every question
